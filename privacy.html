Overview

Corporategate Limited ("Corporategate", "we", "us" or "our") is a Nigerian private company limited by shares. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website and in the course of providing our services across procurement, ICT, security, logistics, training and project delivery.

By using this website or submitting an enquiry, quote request or vendor enquiry, you agree to the handling of your information as described here.

Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to respond to you and deliver our services, including:

  • Contact details you provide — name, organisation, email address, phone number.
  • Enquiry details — the service you are interested in, your requirements, messages and any documents you choose to share.
  • Vendor information — company name, supply category and capability details submitted through our vendor enquiry form.
  • Technical data — basic, non-identifying information your browser sends when you visit, used to keep the site working.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to quote requests, vendor enquiries and general messages.
  • Prepare documented proposals and coordinate the delivery of services.
  • Assess vendors and partners for suitability.
  • Meet our legal, regulatory and tender-compliance obligations.
  • Improve our website and the services we offer.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only where necessary — for example with trusted partners, subcontractors or suppliers involved in delivering a service to you, or where required by law, regulation or a competent authority. Anyone we share information with is expected to protect it to a standard consistent with this policy.

Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to fulfil the purpose it was collected for, to maintain a documented record of our engagements, and to meet legal and compliance requirements. When it is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

Subject to applicable Nigerian data-protection law, you may request to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop using it for a particular purpose. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We apply reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so while we work to protect your information we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at our email or through our contact page.
