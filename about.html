Company Profile

One partner across eight coordinated divisions

Corporategate Limited is a Nigerian private company limited by shares, incorporated in 2007. Our memorandum sets out a deliberately broad operating scope — general contracts, marketing, import/export, distribution, transport, manufacturing and industrial supply, ICT, telecommunications, security systems, training, management systems, construction and investments.

In practice, that breadth lets us act as a single accountable partner: sourcing what you need, integrating the technology around it, moving the goods, and delivering the project — all with documentation built for audit and tender review.
